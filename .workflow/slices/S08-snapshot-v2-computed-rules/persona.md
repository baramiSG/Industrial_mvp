PROHIBITED SHORTCUTS: authoring outcomes in the snapshot (no `rule_context`, no per-rule `fired` fields); fabricating partner rows or production/retained quantities; changing `thresholds.v1.yaml` or `sector_profiles.v1.yaml`; editing `data/synthetic/**`; altering golden expectations; disabling boundary tests; skipping the pre/post migration equivalence proof; regenerating baselines outside the canonical container or before the functional suite is green; editing the DOCX.
