# Ministry opportunity assessment — v3

The missing connection is **record-derived company discovery and preliminary capability assessment**, followed by a properly scoped line comparison. The existing engine remains useful; the current implementation does not yet connect all these stages. V2 supplied the comparator but still started from an authored candidate list. V3 makes discovery part of the same demonstration and proposes the small methodology amendment needed to explain record-derived preliminary findings without fabricating engineering distance.

## Business question and available context

The original meeting minutes distinguish prioritisation of roughly 1,300 products from technical definition of shortlisted opportunities. The decisive question is whether an existing manufacturer can meet the actual specification, quantity and timing through qualification or upgrade, or whether another investor is justified. Manufacturing the product family is insufficient. Sources: the original DOCX and its paragraph extract in `../sources/meeting-minutes.*`, especially paragraphs 2–15 and 20–26.

All five supplied Ministry screenshots were inspected in this turn. They present registry, trade, HS–NIS mapping, production actuals, factory customs, tariff/exemptions, application tape, approved-deal economics and establishment/trade data. The screenshots say factory customs has named firms, but the displayed production-actuals description is per HS. They do not prove that production, certificates, customs or applications join to a particular line. V3 models representative joins explicitly; it does not claim the real extracts have that grain. The platform's own “Nothing here is synthetic” claim is not applied to our demonstration. Menu names neither establish nor disprove the unresolved industrial decision capability.

## What the demonstration will and will not establish

With only baseline register rows, the user can see which operating plants have relevant family evidence, which additional signals exist, where the data stops, and what record/action is needed next. An importer is not transformed into a manufacturer; a planned plant is not operating capacity; a missing row is “not found in this extract/window,” not “absent in Saudi Arabia.” Engineering declarations and optional line evidence then allow the stronger comparator and guarded Test2 result.

Steel retains 57.5092 kt reference qualified capacity, 46.4908 kt shortfall, D*=0.2667 and route5's conditional package. PP's legacy 1,170 kt is an aggregate (450+720), not a physical line, and its 80 kt allocation is not transferred to the two candidate lines. These are retained v2/Claude-verified reference facts; this turn did not rerun their calculations. The aluminium shared-enabler example remains available using its existing declared dependencies and unlock-value calculation; no new portfolio/cost-allocation optimiser is proposed.

Evidence coverage states which required observations/declarations are present. It is not empirical accuracy, a probability that the plant can deliver, or validation against real Ministry outcomes. Real factory recommendations require actual records, verified attribution, engineering confirmation and line-specific economics.
